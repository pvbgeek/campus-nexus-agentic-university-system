# Campus Nexus Agentic University System
### Calianna Collins, Dana Steinke, Noelle Evanich, Kavya Sharma, and Parth Bhalerao
### https://github.com/pvbgeek/campus-nexus-agentic-university-system
## Our Proposal
Our project is going to create an agentic system capable of managing the online portal of a university book store. This system will manage student return requests, handle limited amounts student records, and respond to emails and messages about these topics. Our team is divided into two groups, both a red team and a blue team, to coordinate the development and safety of our agent.
